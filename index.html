Scenic Train Journeys

Ideal for hill country routes (Colombo -> Kandy -> Ella -> Badulla). Experience famous viewlines over Nine Arches Bridge.

Budget Friendly: $2 - $15
🚕

Private Call Taxis & Chauffeurs

Best for comfort, families, and custom multi-day island tours. Vehicles range from sedans to spacious mini-vans.

Medium - Luxury: $40 - $90/day
🛺

Tuk-Tuk (Auto Rickshaw)

Perfect for quick local trips, short coastal hops, and authentic city exploration.

Budget: Metered / Negotiable
🚌

Express Highway Buses

Fast connection between major hubs (Colombo, Galle, Matara, Jaffna, Kandy via expressway routes).

Budget: $3 - $10
